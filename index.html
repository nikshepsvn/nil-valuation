11Why it might deserve to be cheap

What kills the thesis

No fees reach the token

The 64 NIL fee goes to the treasury. There is no burn, buyback or fee share, and no tokenomics document yet.

The headline venue arrives in 2027

Hyperliquid can't be reached until Skyglow. V1 is Ethereum only, with price or time conditions on BTC, ETH, SOL and USDT.

Committee trust

Any k nodes can read pending instructions, and nobody would know, until Neon in late 2027. The node set is 31 and mostly young.

Latency

The feed goes to nodes, then Ethereum's 12-second blocks. Reveals have taken about 78 seconds or more. On 10 Oct 2025 Hyperliquid force-closed ~$7.6B in 12 minutes, so a late fill is a bad fill.

Supply

About 19.5M NIL a month until early 2028, with circulating up ~58% by the end of 2027. The token is upgradeable and has a mint role, reportedly held by a 3-of-5 Safe with no timelock.

Competition

Zama (~$880M FDV) could ship encrypted conditional orders. Aster, Paradex and Silhouette already hide orders on their own venues. Fees tend to go to whoever runs matching.

The market chose transparency

Hyperliquid won by being fully public, and privacy-first venues lost share. Regulated flow may stay private on CME, Coinbase and Kalshi servers.

History

A market maker dumped ~65M NIL in Nov 2025, and the story has been reframed four times in 18 months. A new CEO cleaned house in 2026, but that trust still has to be earned back.
